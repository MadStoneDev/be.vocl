he comes up around eleven and i do the thing where i make my breathing go slow and even and let my mouth fall open a bit. and he stands on his side a second — i can hear him deciding — and then the mattress dips and he turns away and that's the night. both of us safe for another night.

i'm not asleep. i'm never asleep. i'm lying there counting his breaths and hating the ceiling.

i know how this is supposed to work. you talk to the person. you say "i miss you" or "i don't know what happened to us" or even just "hey." but the words cost something and the pretending is free, and i'm so tired, and tomorrow there's the early start, and it's always easier to already be asleep.

fourteen years. he's a good man. that's the part nobody warns you about — it can go quiet like this with someone who never did a single thing wrong.
